# Delivery dispute policy — delivery-dispute-v1

Fictional demonstration policy. This version is enforced by the dispute service.

Orders not marked delivered require shipping review. Delivered-order disputes
within seven days may be investigated. Missing or invalid delivery age and missing
or inconclusive delivery proof require further investigation. Conflicting proof
supports a refund review proposal; matching proof requires carrier review.
Disputes beyond seven days are escalated for manual consideration.

Every resolution requires human review. No dispute decision authorizes payment,
guarantees eligibility, or executes a refund. Customer statements alone are not
verified delivery evidence. Monetary approval limits and payment execution are not
implemented in this version.
